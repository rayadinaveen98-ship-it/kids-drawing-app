# 00 — Product Vision

## Vision

Build the most patient, child-friendly digital art teacher we can: a product that teaches drawing and coloring at the child's pace while preserving creativity, confidence and play.

## Product promise

**Draw together with a teacher who never runs out of patience.**

## Product category

Personal Art Teacher + Creative Studio.

## Learning loop

Learn → Watch → Draw Together → Practice → Color → Create Freely → Tell Stories → Grow as an Artist.

## Product principles

### 1. Creativity before compliance
The app teaches technique without implying that there is only one correct picture.

### 2. Assistance without shame
No harsh pass/fail language for children. Assistance escalates gently.

### 3. Age changes the experience
Age influences content, tool density, guidance, language and UI—not merely a recommendation filter.

### 4. The child controls pace
The teacher can slow down, repeat, pause or accelerate without restarting a lesson.

### 5. Serious art underneath simple UX
Young children receive a simple surface backed by an engine capable of growing toward real digital-art tools.

### 6. Safe by architecture
Collect little data, avoid open social systems, and keep core experiences local/offline.
