# 02 — User & Age System

## Design principle

Age is one signal. Demonstrated ability and assistance needs should eventually refine the experience.

## Little Artists — 4–5
- Very large touch targets
- Minimal reading requirement
- Strong narration/visual cueing
- 3–8 step lessons initially
- Finger-friendly tools
- tracing and gross motor control
- simple recognizable subjects
- no exposed layers/settings complexity

## Creative Explorers — 6–7
- 6–15 step drawings
- simple proportions and shape construction
- wider subject choice
- coloring technique basics
- optional hints rather than constant tracing

## Growing Artists — 8–9
- more construction drawing
- expressions/characters/scenery
- introductory shading and proportion
- increased free-draw controls

## Young Artists — 10–12
- perspective basics
- faces and anatomy basics
- lighting/shadow
- more sophisticated brush and layer concepts when ready
- character/environment design

## Handedness

UI controls should avoid the dominant hand covering important teaching visuals where practical.
