# 09 — Companion Specification (Initial)

## Purpose

The companion is the emotional teaching layer, not decoration.

## Initial semantic states

- IDLE
- GREETING
- TEACHING
- WATCHING_CHILD
- WAITING
- ENCOURAGING
- HELPING
- THINKING
- CELEBRATING
- LESSON_COMPLETE

## Rules

- Never obstruct the critical drawing region.
- Reactions come from product/lesson events, not random timers alone.
- Voice and visual expression should reflect the same semantic state.
- Do not shame or overpraise every stroke.
- Quiet presence is valuable; the character need not speak continuously.
