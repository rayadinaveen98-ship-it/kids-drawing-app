# 10 — Child Safety & Privacy Principles

This is a product/engineering baseline, not a substitute for formal legal review before public launch.

## Early product rules

- No public child profiles.
- No open child messaging/chat.
- No stranger social graph.
- No precise location.
- Avoid collecting full birth date when age/age band is enough.
- Prefer nickname over legal name.
- No behavioral advertising design.
- Parent-gate sensitive actions such as export, purchases and external navigation.
- Keep artwork local by default.
- Network/cloud is optional for core use.
- Microphone/voice features must be explicit and narrowly scoped if introduced.
- Third-party SDK count should be minimized.
